Questions

Straight answers.

Am I the right fit?

You own a detailing or protection business in the US. You do $10K a month or more. You sell coating, PPF, tint or wraps, and you have room for more jobs. If you're under $10K a month, don't apply yet. Come back when you get there.

Do I need to be running ads already?

No. Some owners come to us stuck with a bad agency. Some run their own ads and hit a ceiling. Some never figured ads out at all. What you need is a real monthly ad budget and room on your calendar.

How is this different from my last agency?

Most agencies stop at the lead. We don't. Every lead gets a text back in seconds, gets qualified, and gets tracked in your App, so you can see which ad made you money.

And you can check us. Every review on our Trustpilot is five stars, and @leadfuse on Instagram posts real client wins straight from Slack.

How fast will I see results?

Some owners book jobs the first night. Others take a few weeks. I won't promise you a number. How fast you call leads back and how well you close matter as much as the ads. Your clock starts when your campaigns go live, not the day you pay.

What happens if it doesn't work?

We have a performance condition. If your campaigns run 8 weeks at the ad budget we agree on and you have fewer than 50 qualified leads in your App, you get 4 more weeks free.

A qualified lead matches the service and area from your application, has a real name, phone and email, and answered at least one of our texts or calls. It's all tracked in your App, so nobody argues the count. There are no refunds, because we can't control your follow-up or your closing.

How much does it cost?

It depends on which way you work with us. You get the exact price on the call, before you decide anything. Your ad budget is separate. It's paid to Meta, not to us.

Done paying for junk leads?

Answer a few questions. If you're a fit, we'll show you the plan. If you're not, I'll tell you.
