FAQ

FAQ.

Answered the way we answer them on the call.

We don’t take on everyone. A credit card, cash, and a pulse are not the sole qualifiers that grant you access to our service. We’ve worked with over 100 businesses and we know exactly who gets the biggest result, so the guidelines are strict. This is for you if you’re doing at least $25K a month, you sell coatings, PPF, tint, or wraps at $1,000+ per job, you have a team handling the work, you’re already spending on Meta ads, and you’re stuck at the same number every month because somewhere between the ad and the sale, something’s broken and you can’t find it. Could be the ads, the page, the follow-up, or you burned through the “ready to buy“ part of the market. If that’s you, book the call. If it’s not, don’t waste your time or ours.

The corporate answer is “we help local service businesses generate more lead flow through Meta ads.” That answer is why most businesses stay stuck: an offer, an ad, and hope. The actual answer: we find the biggest constraint on your revenue and fix it. The offer, the thing people actually pay for. The ads, the right people on Meta with tested hooks and fresh creative. The follow-up, every lead captured, qualified, and texted back in seconds through the LEADFUSE App. The sales call, the conversation after the lead comes in. The numbers, by service, so you know which lever is holding you back. The goal isn’t more leads, it’s more, better leads: people who can afford $1,000+ jobs, on your calendar every week.

Every agency answers this the same way: “we only work with your industry, we’re the best.” So here are two differences you can verify. One: agencies live off retainers and running your ads is the whole product, so teaching you the skill is the last thing they want. Most of our clients choose to have us show them exactly what we’d do, launch it themselves, and whether they keep us, bring it in-house, or run it alone becomes their choice. Two: instead of calling ourselves the best, here are the receipts. $3.2M+ in tracked client booking revenue in 2025 alone, 100+ client result screenshots on the LEADFUSE Instagram, 100% 5-star on Trustpilot, and 45+ video testimonials from owners we took from $25K and $50K a month to $75K and $100K+ a month. Don’t take our word for it. When you book, our team texts you. Ask them to send you one.

Fck yes. We’re not here to teach you which button to press in Meta, and we don’t launch on an ad account with zero pixel data. We work with owners who already know ads work*, whose problem is growing past the point they’re stuck at. Whether you run them yourself or pay somebody to run them badly, we find the bottleneck keeping you from spending more and making more. The working minimum is $3,000 a month in ad spend, $100 a day, whether we do it for you or teach you. If $100 a day gives you butterflies, this isn’t the service for you.

Our internal goal is to make you back what you paid us within your first week. The first thing we do is an SMS and email blast to your existing customer list, no ad spend needed, and most clients have made their entire investment back from that alone. From there, your clock doesn’t start until your first campaign goes live, so expect us to bug the shit out of you until onboarding is done. Don’t film, approve, or implement, and the clock still moves. We won’t promise a result, because no agency can press a button and make you money. We bring the plan and the leads. Speed depends on how fast you follow up and how well you close. Pushing you to do both is part of what you’re paying us for.

We’re not fans of guarantees. A guarantee is a safety net, and people with safety nets play soft. What we have is one performance condition, because our reputation matters more than any dollar amount, and we walk you through it on the call. A qualified lead is a real person in your area, asking about a service you sell, who responded to at least one touch, all tracked in your dashboard inside the LEADFUSE App, so there’s no arguing the count. No refunds, because we can’t control your follow-up or your closing. If the leads don’t show up, that’s on us. If they do and you don’t work them, that’s on you. Acquisition is our job; an aggressive bias for action is yours.

You get the exact number on the call, once we know your business. Paid in full. Ad spend is on top, minimum $3,000 a month, and that money goes to Meta, not us. Financing is available, but it costs more and we don’t typically recommend it. We want clients who can pay in full and still fund ad spend without sweating, because that’s the position you close from. No bargaining, no discounts. Prices go up consistently, so today’s price is the lowest it will ever be. If the number feels like a lot, you’re not at the level yet to make this decision, and that’s fine. Come back when you are.

Then this isn’t the service for you yet. If you’re under $100 a day, one of two things is true: somebody’s charging you a small retainer to barely manage a campaign you could run yourself, or you haven’t had a big enough reason to commit real money. We exist to make growing businesses grow faster, the polite way of saying we make rich owners richer, and that’s easier when you’ve got some gambling money to play with. Our minimums are minimum. $3,000 a month shouldn’t be a stretch, it should be an “okay, let’s see what happens.” When that $3,000 makes you money, we grow the spend, because now spending more means making more. Not there yet? Go get to $25K a month and come back. We’ll be more expensive, but you’ll be ready.
