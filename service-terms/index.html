6. The 4-Week Test Drive: extra rules

These rules exist because the Test Drive gives you a lot of protection, and they keep that protection fair for both sides.

  • Your side: film within 7 days of your launch call; spend at least $50 a day on ads every day for 4 weeks straight (a pause of more than 24 hours that you caused breaks this); call or text every lead back within 30 minutes during business hours; mark every booked job in the App; show up to all 4 weekly calls.

  • Performance guarantee: if you did everything on your side and your App shows fewer qualified leads than the number in your Agreement (25 unless we agree on another number) at the end of week 4, ask in writing within 7 days of your week 4 call and we refund your full deposit within 7 days.

  • After a refund the ads we built are turned off, and the scripts, edited videos, ad copy and offer we made for you stay ours. You can’t keep running them. Your raw footage, leads and customer list stay yours.
  • Keeping going after week 4 moves you into the program and price in your Agreement, with your deposit credited.
