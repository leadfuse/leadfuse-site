How we hire

Four Steps. No Shortcuts.

Here's exactly what happens after you apply.

  1. 1

    Apply here, with a short Loom.

    Answer a few questions and record a Loom. 3 minutes, max. It's the first thing I watch.

  2. 2

    Join a group call.

    If you're a fit, we email you an invite. You hear how we work and how you're paid, and you can ask anything.

  3. 3

    1-on-1 with a live test.

    A roleplay or a test task, based on your role. I give you feedback and you run it again. I'm watching one thing: do you use the feedback?

    Paid trial.

    You start on a paid ramp with clear goals. By the end, we both know if it's a fit.

Questions

Before You Apply.

Is it remote?

Yes. The whole team is remote. Most seats work US business hours, because that's when shop owners pick up.

Do I need experience?

It helps. It's not what we hire on. We hire people who take feedback and use it. We can teach the rest.

What's the Loom?

A short video you record with Loom. It's free at loom.com. 3 minutes max. Answer the 3 questions for your role. The form shows them to you. Set the link so anyone with it can watch.

Anything I should know before I apply?

One thing. The last question asks for a subject line. Type it exactly like this, with your real name: Your Role | Your Name | Stage 1 Application. For example: Phone Setter | Maria Lopez | Stage 1 Application. Skip it and we don't call back.

When will I hear back?

We read every application. If you're a fit, we email you an invite to a group call. If your role is in the talent pool, we keep your application and reach out when a seat opens.
